## Abstract (paraphrased)

The canonical multi-segment broken power-law IMF, with slopes $\alpha_0 = 0.3$ (brown dwarfs), $\alpha_1 = 1.3$ ($0.08$–$0.5\,\Msun$), $\alpha_2 = 2.3$ ($0.5$–$1\,\Msun$), $\alpha_3 = 2.3$ ($> 1\,\Msun$). Continuity coefficients enforced at each break.
