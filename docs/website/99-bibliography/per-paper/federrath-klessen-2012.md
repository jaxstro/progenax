```{admonition} The star formation rate of turbulent magnetized clouds
:class: note

**Reference.** *The Astrophysical Journal* **761, 156** (2012).

```

## Abstract (paraphrased)

Density PDF in supersonic-turbulent self-gravitating molecular clouds is well-described by a lognormal+power-law form. SFR follows a $\rho^{3/2}$ kernel (FDF). $\sigma_s^2 = \ln(1 + b^2 \mathcal{M}^2)$ with $b \in [1/3, 1]$.
