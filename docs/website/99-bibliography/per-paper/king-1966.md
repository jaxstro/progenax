# King (1966)

```{admonition} The structure of star clusters. III. Some simple dynamical models
:class: note

**Authors.** I. R. King

**Reference.** *The Astronomical Journal* **71, 64** (1966).

**DOI.** [10.1086/109857](https://doi.org/10.1086/109857)
```

## Abstract (paraphrased)

Introduces the lowered-isothermal distribution function and the resulting tidally-truncated density profile. The DF is a Maxwellian truncated at the tidal energy $E = \Phi_t$, with a critical lowering subtraction that removes the high-energy tail. Solving the Poisson equation self-consistently yields the King density profile, parameterised by the dimensionless central potential $W_0$.
