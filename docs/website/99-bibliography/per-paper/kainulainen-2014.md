## Abstract (paraphrased)

Observational analysis of column-density PDFs from 16 nearby molecular clouds, deriving the median radial-profile slope $p \approx 5/3 = 1.67$. Provides the strongest observational anchor for PP20: $\zeta(p = 1.67) \approx 1.79$.
