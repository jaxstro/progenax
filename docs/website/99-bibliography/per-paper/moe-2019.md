# Moe, Kratter & Badenes (2019)

```{admonition} The close binary fraction of solar-type stars is strongly anticorrelated with metallicity
:class: note

**Authors.** M. Moe, K. M. Kratter, C. Badenes

**Reference.** *The Astrophysical Journal* **875, 61** (2019).

**DOI.** [10.3847/1538-4357/ab0d88](https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-4357/ab0d88)
```

## Abstract (paraphrased)

The close-binary fraction of solar-type stars varies strongly with metallicity, from $\sim 53\%$ at $\mathrm{[Fe/H]} = -3$ to $\sim 10\%$ at $\mathrm{[Fe/H]} = +0.5$. Important for binary population synthesis at low metallicity.
