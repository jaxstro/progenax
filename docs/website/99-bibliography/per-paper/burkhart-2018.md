# Burkhart (2018)

```{admonition} The star formation rate in the gravoturbulent ISM
:class: note

**Authors.** B. Burkhart

**Reference.** *The Astrophysical Journal* **863, 118** (2018).

**DOI.** [10.3847/1538-4357/aad002](https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-4357/aad002)
```

## Abstract (paraphrased)

Combines lognormal density PDF with a power-law tail, integrating the FDF kernel to predict cloud-integrated SFR vs Mach, forcing parameter, and tail slope $\alpha$. Establishes the closed-form transition density $s_t$. Part I of the BM19 framework.
