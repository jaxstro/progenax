# Sana et al. (2012)

```{admonition} Binary interaction dominates the evolution of massive stars
:class: note

**Authors.** H. Sana et al.

**Reference.** *Science* **337, 444** (2012).

**DOI.** [10.1126/science.1223344](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1223344)
```

## Abstract (paraphrased)

Spectroscopic survey of $\sim 70$ O-type stars in 6 young Galactic open clusters establishing that $\sim 70\%$ of all massive stars will undergo significant binary interaction. Provides the empirical period distribution for OB-type primaries: a broken-power-law in $\log P$ with strong short-period bias.
