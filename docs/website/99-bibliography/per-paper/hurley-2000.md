# Hurley, Pols & Tout (2000)

```{admonition} Comprehensive analytic formulae for stellar evolution as a function of mass and metallicity
:class: note

**Authors.** J. R. Hurley, O. R. Pols, C. A. Tout

**Reference.** *Monthly Notices of the Royal Astronomical Society* **315, 543** (2000).

**DOI.** [10.1046/j.1365-8711.2000.03426.x](https://doi.org/10.1046/j.1365-8711.2000.03426.x)
```

## Abstract (paraphrased)

Comprehensive analytic fits to detailed stellar-evolution tracks across a wide range of mass and metallicity. Underlies many population synthesis codes.

## Use in progenax

- Historical stellar-evolution background. The released ZAMS labels use
  [Tout et al. (1996)](tout-1996.md), not a full SSE evolution pipeline.
- Tidal scaling intuition belongs to [Hurley et al. (2002)](hurley-2002.md)
  and the eccentricity page; no BSE tidal-evolution ODE is integrated here.

## Notes

This abstract-level note does not establish an implemented full stellar- or
binary-evolution model in Progenax.
