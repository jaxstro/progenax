# Prša et al. (2016)

```{admonition} Nominal values for selected solar and planetary quantities: IAU 2015 Resolution B3
:class: note

**Authors.** Andrej Prša, Petr Harmanec, Guillermo Torres, Eric Mamajek, et al. (IAU
Inter-Division A–G Working Group on Nominal Units for Stellar & Planetary Astronomy).

**Reference.** *The Astronomical Journal* **152**, 41 (7 pp.), 2016 August.

**DOI.** [10.3847/0004-6256/152/2/41](https://doi.org/10.3847/0004-6256/152/2/41) ·
**ADS.** [2016AJ....152...41P](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2016AJ....152...41P)
```

## What it provides

IAU 2015 Resolution B3 fixes a set of **nominal** conversion constants so that solar and
planetary quantities are reported against exact, agreed reference values — decoupling them
from the slowly-improving *measured* constants. The quantity progenax depends on is the
**nominal solar mass parameter**,

$$(\mathcal{GM})_\odot^{\mathrm N} = 1.3271244\times10^{20}\ \mathrm{m^3\,s^{-2}},$$

reported alongside nominal solar radius, luminosity, and effective temperature, and the
analogous nominal terrestrial and Jovian quantities.

## Use in progenax

- `progenax.analytical.base` — the solar-mass **unit** underlying `SOLAR_SYSTEM_PLANETS`
  is this IAU 2015 nominal mass parameter. (The planet/Sun mass *ratios* in that table are
  IAU 2009 / Luzum et al. 2011, verified separately — see
  [](../../50-validation/analytical-test-cases.md).)
