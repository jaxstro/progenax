# Analytical test cases validation

progenax's analytical IC builders (`progenax.analytical`) emit exact-solution initial
conditions whose defining invariants can be checked directly. Test file:
`tests/validation/test_analytical_physics.py` (**12 tests**); figures:
`scripts/validate_analytical.py`. The orbits are integrated in-script with symplectic
velocity-Verlet (the scheme the tests use); the **oracle is always the analytic
invariant** — the energy formula, the figure-eight's $L=0$ and closure, Kepler's third
law, the harmonic solution — not the integrator.

:::{admonition} Key result: exact ICs reproduce their analytic invariants
:class: important
The two-body builder returns $E = -Gm_1m_2/2a$ to machine precision and an ellipse
that closes after one period (residual $1\times10^{-7}$). The Chenciner–Montgomery
figure-eight has **exactly zero** total angular momentum and closes to $4\times10^{-8}$.
The eight-planet table reproduces Kepler's third law $T^2\propto a^3$ against the
*observed* sidereal periods to $0.7\%$, and the IAU 2009 mass ratios to $\le10^{-4}$.
A plausible-but-wrong figure-eight (a spurious net rotation, $L\neq0$) is correctly
caught — it drifts and does not close.
:::

## What is verified

Rows map to `test_analytical_physics.py`; **Measured** values are regenerated by
`scripts/validate_analytical.py`.

```{list-table}
:header-rows: 1

* - Property
  - Tolerance (as tested)
  - Measured
  - Anchor
* - Two-body energy $E = -Gm_1m_2/2a$
  - $< 10^{-12}$ (indep. of $e$)
  - exact ($-0.0500$)
  - Kepler / vis-viva
* - Two-body Kepler III period
  - $< 10^{-10}$ rel
  - exact
  - $T = 2\pi\sqrt{a^3/GM}$
* - Eccentric ellipse closes ($e=0.5$)
  - $< 10^{-4}$ after 1 period
  - $1.1\times10^{-7}$
  - bound Kepler orbit
* - Figure-eight total $L$
  - $< 10^{-10}$
  - $0$ (exact)
  - Chenciner–Montgomery $L=0$
* - Figure-eight closes
  - $< 10^{-6}$ after 1 period
  - $4.3\times10^{-8}$
  - choreography periodicity
* - Figure-eight COM/momentum
  - $0$ to $10^{-8}$
  - $\le10^{-8}$
  - barycentric, $\sum m\mathbf v=0$
* - Kepler III across 8 planets
  - rel $< 10^{-2}$ vs observed $T$
  - max $6.9\times10^{-3}$
  - JPL Horizons $a$ + observed $T$
* - Planet mass ratios (IAU 2009)
  - rel $< 2\times10^{-3}$
  - $332945$ / $1047.4$ / $3498$
  - Earth / Jupiter / Saturn
* - Harmonic $x(t)=A\cos(\omega t+\phi)$
  - max dev $< 10^{-3}$
  - $5.7\times10^{-7}$
  - SHM exact solution
* - Harmonic energy $E=\tfrac12 m\omega^2A^2$
  - $\Delta E/E < 10^{-4}$
  - $5.6\times10^{-7}$
  - SHM invariant
* - Adversarial: spun figure-eight fails
  - $L\neq0$ and no closure
  - $L=0.30$, closure $2.1$
  - wrong IC correctly rejected
```

:::{note} Provenance
The figure-eight initial conditions are Chenciner & Montgomery (2000), *Ann. Math.*
**152**, 881, with the Simó (2001) numerical coefficients (primary PDF not held; the IC
is validated *by integration* — closure + $L=0$ — exactly as the test does). The
planet orbital elements are JPL Horizons (J2000.0); the mass ratios are checked against
the IAU 2009 nominal values. Kepler's laws and the SHM solution are textbook.
:::

**The Chenciner–Montgomery figure-eight.** Three equal masses chase one another along a
single figure-eight curve (grey); the markers are the canonical collinear start. The

**Two-body Kepler.** (a) An $e=0.5$ relative orbit integrated one period returns to its

well inside tolerance: the energy equals $-Gm_1m_2/2a$ to machine precision, and
$\Delta E/E$ and $\Delta|L|$ over the period are far below their thresholds.

**Kepler's third law across the solar system.** (a) The eight planets (JPL $a$, observed

Kepler-predicted period matches the observed period to $\le0.7\%$ for every planet (the

:::{figure} figures/analytical_harmonic.png

**Simple harmonic oscillator.** (a) The integrated trajectory tracks the exact

:label: val-an-adversarial

**Why the IC must be exact (adversarial).** (a) The canonical figure-eight closes with
$L=0$. (b) A plausible mistake — adding a spurious solid-body rotation to the velocities

bodies drift apart and never close. This is the failure mode the closure + $L=0$ tests
are designed to catch.
:::

## How to run

```bash
# exact-solution physics tests (12 tests, ~2 s on CPU)
pytest tests/validation/test_analytical_physics.py -q

# regenerate the five figures with PASS/FAIL tables
python scripts/validate_analytical.py
```

## What this suite does *not* test

- **Long-term integrator stability** — progenax's analytical builders only *provide*
  ICs; long-term N-body integration and its conservation are gravax's responsibility.
  (The in-script Verlet here runs for a single period purely to expose the invariants.)
- **Relativistic corrections** — Newtonian gravity only.

## References

Chenciner & Montgomery (2000), *Ann. Math.* **152**, 881 and Simó (2001) for the
figure-eight; JPL Horizons (J2000.0) and the IAU 2009 nominal masses for the solar
system; standard celestial-mechanics texts for Kepler and the harmonic oscillator. The
builders are in `progenax/analytical/`.
